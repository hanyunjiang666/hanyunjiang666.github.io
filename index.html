About Me

I am a second-year graduate student working on AI for Code, with a particular focus on code optimization and code agents, under the supervision of Prof. Peisen Yao. My research aims to develop intelligent agents capable of autonomously understanding, reasoning about, and improving software systems through a wide range of code-centric tasks. Feel free to reach out if you'd like to collaborate with me.
